<p xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="shortdesc">This document shows you how to add or remove <a className="xref" href="/katalon-testops/administration-and-licensing/manage-systems/permissions/about-roles#concept-3410__about-project-level-roles">Project Roles</a> from a Member. </p> 
<div xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="section prereq p"><ul className="ul"><li className="li"><p className="p">Make sure you are a Project Admin or possess the relevant permissions. <section xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="section context">Edit a Member's Project Roles by going to <span className="ph menucascade"><span className="ph uicontrol">Admin Settings</span><abbr title="and then"> &gt; </abbr><span className="ph uicontrol">Project</span><abbr title="and then"> &gt; </abbr><span className="ph uicontrol">Members</span></span> and clicking on the <span className="ph uicontrol">Edit</span> icon within their row. </section>
