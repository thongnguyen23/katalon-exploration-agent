# <a id="task-2123" class="anchor_top_offset"/><a id="ariaid-title1" class="anchor_top_offset"/>Delete an application

<section xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="section context">Go to the <span className="ph uicontrol">Application Repository</span> to delete your app.</section> 
<ol xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="ol steps"><li className="li step"><span className="ph cmd">Go to <span className="ph uicontrol">Resources</span> &gt; <span className="ph uicontrol">Application</span>. Enter the application name you want to delete in the search bar.</span></li><li className="li step"><span className="ph cmd">Click the three-dot menu at the far right of your chosen app.</span></li><li className="li step"><span className="ph cmd">Select <span className="ph uicontrol">Delete</span>.</span></li></ol> 
<section xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="section result">You have deleted your application.</section>
