# <a id="task-9355" class="anchor_top_offset"/><a id="ariaid-title1" class="anchor_top_offset"/>Edit your custom domain

<div xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="section prereq p"><ul className="ul"><li className="li"><p className="p">You must have <a className="xref" href="/katalon-platform/administer/administration-tasks/security-settings/subdomain-configurations">configured a subdomain. </a></p></li></ul><ul className="ul"><li className="li"><p lang="en" className="p">Make sure you are an Account Admin or possess the relevant permissions. <section xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="section context">You can change your subdomain name within the General tab in System. </section> 
<ol xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="ol steps"><li className="li step stepexpand"><span className="ph cmd">Go to <span className="ph menucascade"><span className="ph uicontrol">Admin Settings</span><abbr title="and then"> &gt; </abbr><span className="ph uicontrol">System</span></span>. Enter your desired value that falls within the following parameters:</span><div className="itemgroup info"><ul className="ul"><li className="li"><p className="p">Limit it to 63 characters</p></li><li className="li"><p className="p">Use a-z letters in any casing</p></li><li className="li"><p className="p">Use numbers 0-9</p></li><li className="li"><p className="p">Use a hyphen but never twice consecutively</p></li></ul></div></li><li className="li step stepexpand"><span className="ph cmd">Click <span className="ph uicontrol">Save changes</span>.</span></li></ol> 
<section xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="section result">You have changed your subdomain name successfully. </section>
