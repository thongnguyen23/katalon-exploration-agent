Way of Working (ontology branch)

- Follow AGENTS.md steps 0–10 for each iteration.
- Source of truth: `specs/ontology/SPEC.md` and the codebase.
- Keep changes minimal and reversible; avoid touching shared tools.
- Validation first: run the builder on a small docs slice, then expand.
- Logging: no secrets; record paths and counts in `JOURNAL.md`.

Per-iteration checklist
- Update `TODO.md` (next thin slice) before and after work.
- Keep `SPEC_PROGRESS.md` in sync with reality.
- If blocked on decisions, write them to `FOR_HUMAN.md` and continue with parallel safe tasks.
