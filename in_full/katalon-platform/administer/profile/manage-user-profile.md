Learn how you can manage your user profile in Katalon TestOps.

---

## View your user profile

To view your user profile, follow these steps:

1. Log in to Katalon TestOps. If you possess the Member role, you are taken directly to it.

2. If you are not a Member, then click on the **Profile Picture** in the top-right corner of the page.

3. Click on **User Profile** to view your profile details.

<br/>

<img src="https://tw-cdn.katalon.com/katalon-testops/my-profile/Profile User Profile Page 2.png" alt="Your Account information in Katalon TestOps" width="1080"/>

<br/>

Here you can see all relevant information about your account, including your personal details, Account information, Organization information, license information, and Project information.

- **Project Tab**: This tab displays all the Projects you are a member of, as well as your general details for each.
- **Account Tab**: This tab displays the Accounts you have access to.
- **Change History**: Coming soon.
- **Activity History**: Coming soon.

## Edit your user profile

1. To change your details such as your:

    - Profile picture
    - Working title 

    Click on **Edit Information**. A corresponding dialog box appears and you can enter your information as needed. 

2. Once done, click on **Save** to save your changes.


## Change your password

1. Click on the **Change Password** button. A corresponding dialog box appears.

2. Input your current password.

3. Input your new password twice. 

4. Click **Change** to save your changes.
