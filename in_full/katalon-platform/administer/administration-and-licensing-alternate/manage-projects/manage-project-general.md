## Manage Project General

To manage the details of your project, such as its name, description, and administrators, follow these steps:

1. Go to **Settings** > **General**. The Project General settings page appears.

<br/>

<img src="https://tw-cdn.katalon.com/katalon-testops/Settings/General/Settings Project General.png" alt="Your Project General information in Katalon TestOps" width="1080"/>

<br/>

2. Optional: Click on the text box labeled **Project Name** to edit the name of your project.

3. Optional: Click on the text box labeled **Project Description** to edit the description of your project.

4. Optional: Click on the dropdown menun labeled **Project Admin** to add or remove Project Admins.

    Add a Project Admin by ticking the checkbox next to their name, and remove them by unticking the checkbox. Once done, click on **Save**.
