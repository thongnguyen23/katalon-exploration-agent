# <a id="concept-9818" class="anchor_top_offset"/><a id="ariaid-title1" class="anchor_top_offset"/>Use TestCloud private mobile devices

<div xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="p">TestCloud provides you with a wide range of real mobile devices, in both iOS and Android, to execute mobile tests. With TestCloud private mobile devices, you can have more control over your test devices compared to shared devices:<ul className="ul"><li className="li"><strong className="ph b">Non-shared environment</strong>: Private devices are dedicated to your organization, ensuring high availability and consistent performance.</li><li className="li"><strong className="ph b">Enhanced data privacy</strong>: With complete control over the data stored on the device, you reduce the risk of exposing this data to other users or third parties.</li><li className="li"><strong className="ph b">Session retention</strong>: Private devices come with longer session retention, allow you first configure device functionalities and later perform multiple tests without having to reconfigure from scratch.</li></ul></div>
<div xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="p">With a TestCloud private device subscription, you can perform tests on private mobile devices just as you would on shared devices: <ul className="ul"><li className="li"><a className="xref" href="/katalon-testcloud/mobile-native-app-testing/mobile-native-app-testing-with-testcloud">Run mobile native application tests</a></li><li className="li"><a className="xref" href="/katalon-testcloud/web-testing/mobile-browser-testing-with-testcloud">Run mobile browser tests</a></li></ul></div>
<p xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="p">In Katalon Studio, when selecting device in the <span className="ph uicontrol">TestCloud Configuration</span> dialog, you can find the private device at the top of the list with the UDID.</p> 
<p xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="p"><img className="image" width={500} src={useBaseUrl("/fdc82d23-441c-4fd1-b3ac-67098cfaaeec/private-device-in-ks.png")} alt="Private device in Katalon Studio" /></p> 
<p xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="p">In TestOps, when selecting device in the <span className="ph uicontrol">Configure Test Environment</span> section, you can find your private mobile devices labeled with their UDID.</p> 
<p xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="p"><img className="image" width={600} src={useBaseUrl("/9086408d-984b-4ba9-84c2-d0ac2ca0f4d0/TestCloud_test_scheduler_private_mobile_device.png")} alt="Configure test environment for test suite, device with UDID tag" /></p>
