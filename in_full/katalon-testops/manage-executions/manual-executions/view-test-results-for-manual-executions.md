title: View test results for manual executions
---

This document shows you how to view test results for manual test cases.

## Prerequisites

- Make sure you have the Test Lead or Tester role. Go to [roles](/katalon-testops/administration-and-licensing/manage-systems/permissions/about-roles) or [permissions](/katalon-testops/administration-and-licensing/manage-systems/permissions/about-permissions) for more information or learn how to [assign a Project Admin here](/katalon-testops/administration-and-licensing/manage-administrators/assign-or-unassign-project-administrators).
- Your test cases must be [in a test suite](/katalon-studio/manage-test-artifacts/manage-test-suites-in-katalon-studio#task-39).

## Overview

Gain an overview of your test case's results by using **View Results** feature.

## View test results for manual executions

1. Go to **Executions**. The **Automated** tab appears by default.
2. Click on the **Manual** tab. A list of test suites appears. Click on one.
3. Click on **View Results** at the upper right corner of the screen.

![View Results button location](/0931479b-4c2d-4643-b61c-18b35b0e46f3/TO3GA3_TestPak_Enter_Results.png)

## Results

The Test Case Results screen in TestPak shows a list of your test cases and a summary of their results.
