# Purchase TestOps Licenses

## Prerequisites

- Make sure you are an Account Admin or possess the relevant permissions. Go to [roles](/katalon-testops/administration-and-licensing/manage-systems/permissions/about-roles) or [permissions](/katalon-testops/administration-and-licensing/manage-systems/permissions/about-permissions) for more information or learn how to [assign an Account Admin here](/katalon-testops/administration-and-licensing/manage-administrators/assign-or-unassign-account-administrators).

--- 

## Purchase TestOps Licenses

Purchase additional licenses from **Admin Settings > Account > License Management** to scale operations and allow more users to access Katalon TestOps.

1. Go to **Admin Settings > Account > License Management**. You can find Admin Settings at the upper right corner of the page, indicated by a cog icon.

2. Click on **Purchase License** at the upper right corner of the page.

<br/>

<img src="https://tw-cdn.katalon.com/katalon-testops/Administration and Licensing/Manage Accounts/License Management/License Management Purchase License.png" alt="Your Account information in Katalon TestOps" width="1080"/>

<br/>

 A new tab opens, taking you to the Subscription Management page.

<br/>

<img src="https://tw-cdn.katalon.com/katalon-testops/Administration and Licensing/Manage Accounts/License Management/Purchase TestOps Licenses Step 2.5" alt="Your Account information in Katalon TestOps" width="1080"/>

<br/>

3. Select the licenses you would like to avail of, then click **Checkout**.

4. Confirm your order and your payment details, then click **Checkout** again.

5. A page confirms your purchase and displays your payment status.

<br/>

<img src="https://tw-cdn.katalon.com/katalon-testops/Administration and Licensing/Manage Accounts/License Management/Purchase TestOps Licenses Step 5.png" alt="Your Account information in Katalon TestOps" width="1080"/>

<br/>

## Results

You will receive an email when the payment completes and receive your licenses immediately.
