# Create an Organization or sub-Organization
Learn how you can create an Organization or sub-Organization in Katalon TestOps.

## Prerequisites

- Make sure you are an Account Admin or possess the relevant permissions. Go to [roles](/katalon-testops/administration-and-licensing/manage-systems/permissions/about-roles) or [permissions](/katalon-testops/administration-and-licensing/manage-systems/permissions/about-permissions) for more information or learn how to [assign an Account Admin here](/katalon-testops/administration-and-licensing/manage-administrators/assign-or-unassign-account-administrators).

---

Create an Organization or sub-Organization via the **+ Create** button in the **Organization Management** tab within the **Organization** section.

## Create an Organization or sub-Organization

1. Go to **Admin Settings > Organization**. You can find Admin Settings at the upper right corner of the page, indicated by a cog icon.

    The Organization Management tab appears by default.

2. Click on **+ Create**.

3. Select Organization from the dropdown.

<br/>

<img src="https://tw-cdn.katalon.com/katalon-testops/Administration and Licensing/Manage Organizations/Organization Management/Create Organization.png" alt="Your Account information in Katalon TestOps" width="500"/>

<br/>

4. The **Create Organization dialog box** appears. Input your organization name.

5. In the section below, select your Account or Organization as the parent for the Organization you are creating.

    :::note     
    You can nest organizations within existing ones.
    :::

6. Click **Create**.

## Result

A notification confirms that you've created your Organization or sub-Organization. It is immediately displayed in the organization directory.
