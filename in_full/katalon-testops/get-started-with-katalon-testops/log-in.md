Once you have [created an account at Katalon](https://login.katalon.com/realms/katalon/login-actions/registration?client_id=katalon-testops-gen3&tab_id=D03ugbeA3FY&client_data=eyJydSI6Imh0dHBzOi8vcGxhdGZvcm0ua2F0YWxvbi5jb20vYXV0aC9jYWxsYmFjayIsInJ0IjoiY29kZSIsInN0IjoiNDU1MTIyOTE0MDdlNDRhOTg1MzMwYmIxNzRmOGVhZGYifQ), there are multiple ways with which you can log in to Katalon TestOps.

- Continue with Google
- Continue with GitHub 
- Continue with email
- Continue with SSO

## Login with Google, Github, or Email

Login with any of these credentials [here](https://prodgen3platform.katalon.io/).

<br/>

<img src="https://tw-cdn.katalon.com/katalon-testops/Other/TestOps Domain Access Options.pngg" alt="Log in to Katalon TestOps" width="1080"/>

<br/>

If you have a paid TestOps account or have already set up a custom domain, you are prompted to choose which one you would like to log in to.

<br/>

<img src="https://tw-cdn.katalon.com/katalon-testops/Other/TestOps Domain Access Options.png" alt="Log in to Katalon TestOps" width="1080"/>

<br/>

Choosing any option and clicking **Continue** will bring you to the TestOps Home page.

## Login with SSO

When logging in with SSO, you are prompted to enter your Katalon domain.

<br/>

<img src="https://tw-cdn.katalon.com/katalon-testops/Other/TestOps Katalon Domain .png" alt="Log in to Katalon TestOps" width="1080"/>

<br/>

Then depending on the login option set by your Admin, log in either with SSO or with your username and password, then click Log in. This brings you to the TestOps Home page.
